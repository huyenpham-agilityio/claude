# Household Expenses: overview

Household Expenses is a small web app for one person to track spending, budgets,
income and savings.

## What it does
- Record income and expenses, each with a category, an amount and a date.
- Set a monthly budget per expense category and see how much is used.
- Track savings goals and how much to put aside each month to reach them.
- See reports: totals per month and spending per category, with CSV export.

## Who it is for
A single household. Registration can be closed once the owner's account exists.

## Languages
The app works in English and Vietnamese. Dates and money follow the language.
